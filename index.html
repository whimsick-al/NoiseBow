NoiseBow

your rainbow of noise colours

Audio saves as 5 whole loops (~55–60 s) that repeat seamlessly. WAV is best for looping; MP3 adds a small gap at the loop point. Keep the volume low: deep presets can stress small speakers. MP3 encoding by lamejs (LGPL, lame.sourceforge.net), loaded on demand.
